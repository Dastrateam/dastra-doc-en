# Referentials

To reduce data entry errors, the Dastra record lets you manage one or more referentials completely autonomously and independently from the record. The diagram below illustrates the reusability of the record of processing activities.

![Diagram illustrating the relationships between processing activities and referentials (assets and data processors)](<../../../.gitbook/assets/register-referentiel-architecture-diagram.png>)

## List of referentials offered by Dastra

* **Assets**: assets bring together the elements useful for managing data (such as data storage media, premises, tangible or intangible items). &#x20;
* **Actors**: actors are differentiated from users because they don't necessarily need to log in to the platform.
* **Datasets**: datasets are groups of data forming coherent sets.&#x20;
* **Data**: data are pieces of information. They are the most atomic level in Dastra.
* **Measures**: security measures are technical or organizational safeguards
* **Data subject categories**: these group together categories of individuals&#x20;
