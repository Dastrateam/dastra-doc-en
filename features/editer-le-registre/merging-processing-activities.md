# Merging processing activities

### 🧠 Introduction

**Merging processing activities** lets you **combine several data processing activities into one**, while **keeping the essential information** and the associated history.\
This feature is particularly useful for **streamlining the record of processing activities**, avoiding duplicates, and improving **documentary consistency**.

> 🎯 Goal: simplify record governance by consolidating similar or redundant processing activities.

***

### ⚙️ Enabling the feature

Merging processing activities is available to **administrators** and **contributors** with editing rights on the record.

#### To enable it:

1. Go to the **Record of processing activities** module.
2. Select **several processing activities** to merge.
3. Click **Merge**.
4. Choose:
   * The **main processing activity** (the one that will be kept),
   * The fields to **prioritize** or **combine** (e.g. purposes, data categories, legal bases).
5. Confirm the merge to create the **new consolidated processing activity**.

> 💡 Merged processing activities are automatically archived to guarantee traceability and reversibility of the operation.

***

### 🚀 Key features

#### 🔍 1. Data merging and analysis

Dastra lets you **combine different processing activities** and their **associated content** (data categories, retention periods, security measures, data processors, etc.).\
The tool automatically detects:

* **similar fields** to merge,
* **diverging values** to resolve manually,
* and **potential inconsistencies** between processing activities.

> This makes it easier to analyze, consolidate, and produce a **consistent map** of your GDPR record.

***

#### 🧩 2. Integrated and centralized management

All the information from merged processing activities is grouped into a **unified record**, including:

* **Consolidated purposes**,
* The associated **legal bases**,
* **Categories of data subjects and data**,
* **Transfers and data processors**,
* **Security measures**,
* **Linked documents** (DPIA, policies, evidence...).

> 🎯 This centralization simplifies record management and ensures consistency across departments or entities.

***

#### ⚙️ 3. Process automation

Merging relies on Dastra's automation capabilities:

* Automatic detection of duplicates and similar processing activities,
* Merge suggestions based on **shared metadata** (purpose, controller, domain, etc.),
* Automatic updates of **cross-references** (links to audits, risks, AI, breaches, etc.),
* Complete logging of actions (traceability, author, date, and justification).

> 🔁 Result: a clean, consistent record aligned with operational reality — without repetitive manual tasks.

***

### 🧠 Typical use cases

| Case                              | Example                                                        | Benefit                                           |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Streamlining**                  | Two processing activities "HR Management" and "Staff Management" | Avoid duplication and simplify maintenance         |
| **Organizational overhaul**       | Merging records after a restructuring or acquisition            | Create a single company-wide record                |
| **Multi-entity harmonization**    | Similar processing activities across subsidiaries               | Facilitate governance and global reporting         |
| **Record cleanup**                | Removing redundancies created during initial imports            | Improve the clarity and reliability of the record  |

***

### 🪄 Best practices

1. **Check compatibility**: only merge processing activities with similar or identical purposes.
2. **Preserve legal consistency**: avoid merging processing activities based on different legal bases.
3. **Document your decisions**: mention the justification in the comments field or in the audit trail.
4. **Test on a limited scope** before generalizing to the entire record.
